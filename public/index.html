King's Gambit

Get your cards to add up to 5 or lower. Peek, swap, match, and knock — 2 to 8 players.

That table doesn't exist any more. Start a new one.

How to play
  • You get 4 face-down cards. At the start you see your bottom two once — remember them.
  • Values: A = 1, 2–10 face value, J/Q = 10, black K (Circle, Chip) = 10, red K (Triangle, Spiral) = −1, Joker = 0.
  • Your turn: draw a card, then swap it with one of yours or discard it.
  • Powers (for the card you put on the pile on your turn — the one you drew, or the one you swapped out): 7/8 look at one of yours · 9/10 look at someone else's · J blind swap two cards from two different players (yours or anyone else's) · Q look at any two · K look at two and swap if you want · Joker shuffles everyone else's cards.
  • Match any time: tap the discard pile, then a card (yours or anyone's) of the same rank. Take someone else's and you give them one of yours. Wrong guess = penalty card. No triple stacks.
  • Knock when you think you're at 5 or lower. Everyone else gets one more turn, and your cards are protected. Others may knock too on their last turn and get the same protection. Then every knocker reveals: over 5 and you're out; if any knocker is at 5 or lower, everyone reveals and the lowest total wins (ties all win). If every knocker busts, play goes on until someone knocks again.
  • Wins are counted on each seat 🏆, and the last winner goes first next round.
